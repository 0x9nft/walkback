## One-line descriptions

See `facts.md` for 13 languages. The shortest English version:

> A free browser life simulator that tells you the year and cause of death first, then walks you backwards through that life to the day you were born.

## Quotable numbers

- 173 countries and territories, weighted by **annual births** (India ≈ 17.7%, China ≈ 6.8%)
- Child mortality before age five: 115.6 per 1,000 in Nigeria versus 2.4 in Japan
- The gap in average life expectancy between the top and bottom of the dataset is about 30 years
- 13 languages, each with its own URL and `<html lang>`
- Four long-form essays; a teacher mode removes social features; no third-party analytics

## What not to write

- It is **not** a prediction, a life-expectancy calculator for the reader, or health advice. Every page says so.
- It is **not** affiliated with 1 in 8 Billion / 80億分の1. Same public data family, opposite chronology, independent build.
- The optional $3.99 keepsake is an album and film. It unlocks nothing.

# walkback

**A free browser life simulator that gives you the ending first.**

> **NOT OPEN SOURCE.** This repository holds documentation and press material only.
> The game's source code, dataset and design system are **not** published here.

You are handed one life drawn from real demographic statistics — a country weighted by annual births, a sex, an age at death, a cause. Then you walk backwards through that life, one year at a time, until you reach the day you were born.

Play it: **https://walkback.live/en**

- 日本語: https://walkback.live/ja
- 简体中文: https://walkback.live/zh
- 한국어: https://walkback.live/ko
- 12 more languages are listed at the bottom of every page.

## Why it exists

Most games in this genre — *1 in 8 Billion* / *80億分の1* / 人生重开模拟器 — run a life from birth to death. walkback uses the same family of public data but reverses the chronology. That one change alters how a year reads: forward, a quiet year at 31 looks like chance; backward, once you already know the life ends at 47, the same line reads as "the year with sixteen left".

It is an independent site. It is not a clone, mirror, sequel or affiliate of 1in8b.

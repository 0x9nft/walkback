# Press facts — walkback

**Site:** https://walkback.live · **Contact:** support@walkback.live

## One-paragraph description (English)

walkback.live is a free browser life simulator that gives you the ending first. A single life is drawn from public demographic statistics — a country weighted by annual births, a sex, an age at death, a cause — and then revealed in reverse: from the final year back, one year at a time, to the day of birth. It uses UN World Population Prospects 2024 revision (estimates through 2023), World Bank WDI and WHO GHO data across 173 countries and territories, in 13 languages. No account, no download, no third-party analytics. The site states on every page that it is a model, not a prediction or health advice. It is an independent project, not affiliated with 1 in 8 Billion / 80億分の1, which uses a similar data family but runs a life from birth to death.

## Data provenance

UN World Population Prospects, **2024 revision** — this is a revision number, not a data year; the estimates run through 2023. Additional indicators from World Bank World Development Indicators and WHO Global Health Observatory. The site deliberately does not chase annual updates: a revision shifts life expectancy by less than the model's own random terms, and what players perceive is the ordering between countries, which barely moves. The revision label is stated on the homepage and game page footers, and in all five disclaimer languages.

## FAQ for a reporter

**Is this a death predictor?** No. It draws a hypothetical life from population statistics. It does not use anything about the reader.

**Why do some lives end at four?** Because child mortality is part of a life table. Removing it would misrepresent the data. The essays explain this explicitly.

**Is it a clone of 1 in 8 Billion?** No — independent build, same public data family, opposite chronology. The reverse order is the entire design idea.

**Is there a commercial side?** One optional $3.99 keepsake (album + film). It unlocks nothing. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

**Who made it?** An independent developer. Interview requests: support@walkback.live
